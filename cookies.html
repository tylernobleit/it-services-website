1. What Cookies and Local Storage Are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as browser local storage, do the same job. We use the word "cookies" in this policy to cover both.

2. What We Use on This Site

Analytics cookies are off until you click Accept on the cookie notice. If you choose Essential only, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting, or Google advertising features, and we do not sell your information.

Some features load from other companies. When your browser fetches them, those companies receive your IP address and basic browser information and may set their own cookies or storage under their own policies.

4. Your Choices

  • Use the Cookie Settings button in the footer to review your choice at any time.
  • Choose Essential only on the notice if you prefer we store nothing beyond that choice. Google Analytics will not load.
  • To change your mind after accepting, use Cookie Settings in the footer and choose Essential only. Google Analytics stops and we clear its cookies. You can also delete cookies in your browser, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works, but your cookie notice choice will not be remembered.
  • Browsers with Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track turned on: we do not track you across sites, so the site behaves the same.

5. If This Changes

If we later add advertising or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent before they run.
